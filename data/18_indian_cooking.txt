Indian Cooking
Indian cooking is famous for its use of spices such as cumin, coriander, turmeric and cardamom. Dishes vary greatly from region to region: the north is known for wheat breads and rich curries, while the south favours rice, lentils and coconut. Cooking often begins by frying whole spices in hot oil to release their aroma. Vegetarian food is especially common, and lentil dishes called dal are eaten almost every day.
