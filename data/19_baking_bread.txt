Baking Bread
Bread is made from flour, water, salt and yeast. When the dough is kneaded, proteins in the flour form gluten, a stretchy network that traps gas. The yeast ferments sugars and produces carbon dioxide, which makes the dough rise. Baking in a hot oven sets the structure, browns the crust and fills the kitchen with a wonderful smell. Sourdough bread uses wild yeast and bacteria instead of commercial yeast.
